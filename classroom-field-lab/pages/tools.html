Tools / Experiments

A gallery of small tools for physics learning and teacher workflow.

These experiments are intentionally small. A useful classroom tool should solve one student problem clearly before it grows.

Classroom-tested

Distance and displacement visualizer

Student problem: Confusing total distance with displacement.

What students do: Drag a path, compare the traveled route with the straight-line change in position, then write a one-sentence distinction.
